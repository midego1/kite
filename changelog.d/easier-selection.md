### Changed

- Selecting mail is quicker and clearer: a checkbox in the list header picks everything on the page (or press Cmd/Ctrl+A), row checkboxes show on hover or with the "Select" button on touch, and once the page is fully checked you can select every message in the folder. The bulk actions now show their names next to their icons, and "Empty Spam"/"Empty Trash" always shows its label.

  ![The Spam list header with Archive, Not spam, Delete, Delete forever and Mark as read shown as labelled buttons above three checked messages](/changelog/2026-11-08-easier-selection-light.webp)

- Opening a message to read it no longer clears a checkbox selection already made elsewhere in the list.
