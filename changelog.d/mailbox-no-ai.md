### Added

- **No AI for this mailbox**: in a mailbox's assistant settings, people who manage it can keep its mail away from AI. Chat, Write with Kite, summaries, suggestions and automatic reading stop there for everyone. Needs a database update applied from the [Admin overview](/admin); see [the assistant docs](https://github.com/midego1/kite/blob/main/docs/assistant.md#no-ai-for-this-mailbox).
