### Changed

- Calendar, Bookings and Drive now use the same frame as the rest of Kite: the mail menu in the sidebar, and the page beside it. Calendar's own top bar is gone; the month, the arrows, **Today**, the view and **New event** sit above the calendar, and the upcoming events are a panel at its left with the Bookings and Calendar tabs on top (a sheet behind an **Upcoming** pill on phones).
  ![Calendar in the app frame in light mode](/changelog/2026-11-02-calendar-frame-light.webp)
  ![Calendar in the app frame in dark mode](/changelog/2026-11-02-calendar-frame-dark.webp)
- Drive's views (My Drive, Recent, Shared with me, All attachments, Trash) are a menu beside the file list, like the Settings menu, with the storage you have used at its foot; the sidebar stays the mail menu. **New folder**, **Upload files** and **Upload folder** are above the list.
  ![Drive in the app frame in light mode](/changelog/2026-11-02-drive-frame-light.webp)
  ![Drive in the app frame in dark mode](/changelog/2026-11-02-drive-frame-dark.webp)
