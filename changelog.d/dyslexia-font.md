### Added

- A dyslexia-friendly font. Under [Settings → Appearance](/settings/appearance), **Font** offers **Dyslexia-friendly (OpenDyslexic)** next to the bundled fonts: the whole app, the composer and plain-text emails switch to OpenDyslexic with a little more room between letters and words, and **Font in HTML emails** can replace the fonts of HTML emails with it as well. The font is self-hosted (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and loaded only while chosen; both choices follow your account to your other devices like the reading layout.
