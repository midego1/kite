### Added

- Send as a connected account's address: once its SMTP sign-in works, the address of a Gmail, iCloud Mail, Yahoo, AOL, Fastmail or other connected account is in the composer's From menu, and mail sent from it goes out through the provider's own SMTP server, so it carries the provider's SPF and DKIM. Replies start from the address the mail came to, the message lands in Sent with its exact bytes, and a copy is saved in the provider's Sent folder (Gmail and Google Workspace keep their own). Undo send, scheduled sends and the sending allowance work as usual. Each account in [Settings → Connected accounts](/settings/connected-accounts) says whether you can send as it, with switches for **Send from this address** and **Save a copy in the provider's Sent folder**. Needs database migration 0083; see [Connected accounts](docs/external-accounts.md#send-as-the-accounts-address).
- A temporary refusal from the provider (`4xx`) is tried again after a minute and then after five; a refused password sets the account to **Sign-in failed**; and a connection that breaks after the message went out fails the send as "Delivery unknown" instead of sending it twice.
