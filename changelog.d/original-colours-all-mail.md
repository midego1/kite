### Fixed

- "Original colours" is offered above every email that dark mode adapts, in every layout and at every width. It used to appear only for mail with a background of its own, so mail that only sets text colours (the reader drops `bgcolor`) was shown with near-invisible dark text and no way back to the sender's colours. The button is small, at the top right of the message body, reachable with the keyboard and shows whether it is on.
