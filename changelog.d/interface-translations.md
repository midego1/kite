### Added

- Kite now speaks Dutch and Arabic. Pick a language under **Language** in the account menu, or leave it on Automatic to follow your browser. Arabic mirrors the interface. The sidebar, message list, reader and composer are translated; settings and admin pages follow later, and the Arabic is still waiting for a native speaker's review. See [Translations](https://github.com/midego1/kite/blob/main/docs/translations.md).
