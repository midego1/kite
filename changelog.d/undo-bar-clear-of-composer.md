### Fixed

- The "Sending in …s" bar with **Send now** and **Undo**, and other notices, no longer cover an open composer. Beside the floating composer they sit to its left, under the expanded composer at the bottom of the screen, and at the top of the screen on a narrow window or on the Compose page. The expanded composer is a little shorter to leave room for them, and the bar fits a phone screen instead of running off its left edge.
