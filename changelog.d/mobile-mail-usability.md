### Added

- A floating "New mail" button on phone and foldable screens, so starting a message no longer takes two taps through the menu.
  ![The inbox on a phone, with a star and a more-actions button beside each row and a floating compose button above the tab bar](/changelog/2026-11-08-mobile-mail-usability.webp)

### Fixed

- Starring, archiving, trashing, snoozing, marking a message read or unread and moving it from the list now work with a tap on any phone, foldable or tablet screen, through a star and a "more actions" button that stay out of each other's way and keep the list at its usual density, instead of needing a mouse hover to even appear.
  ![Four shots of a touch screen: a message row's star and more-actions button side by side, reporting spam now asking to confirm first, the star visible on an unfolded foldable beside the reader, and the reader's toolbar fitting a narrow phone](/changelog/2026-11-08-mobile-mail-review-fixes.webp)
- The reader's toolbar no longer runs off a narrow phone and hides its own "More actions" button; menus, buttons and sidebar controls across the mail app now keep a comfortably tappable size on a touch screen, and a message's date is never hidden behind them.
