### Added

- **One-line summaries in the message list**, off by default: under **Summaries** in the [assistant settings](/inbox), people who manage a mailbox can have Kite write a line of at most 140 characters for new mail and show it in the list instead of the first words, with **Include newsletters and notifications** (off by default) and a daily limit (100 by default). When automatic reading already asks the model about a message, the line comes from that same request, so nothing is summarised twice; otherwise a small request of its own writes it. Spam, Trash, the Screener's held mail, drafts, suspicious and phishing mail, muted senders and short mail never get one, and lines follow automatic reading's switches, external-provider opt-in, pause, burst limit and spend caps. Lines arrive without a reload, are deleted with their message, and each browser can show previews instead under [Settings → Appearance](/settings/appearance). Uses the columns migration **0072** added.
