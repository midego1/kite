### Added

- Kite now believes Google's own verdict on who sent a message for mail synced from a connected Gmail or Google Workspace account, so the spam filter, the sender warning and Money Watch can tell a verified sender from a spoofed one. A new Recheck authentication button on the account, in Settings → Connected accounts, finds that result for recent mail that arrived before this existed.
