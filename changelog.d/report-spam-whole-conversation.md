### Fixed

- Report spam in the reader now takes the whole conversation out of the list in conversation view (the default). It used to report only the open message, so the conversation's other messages stayed in the Inbox and its row kept showing. Report phishing, Archive, Move to trash, Not spam, Move to a folder and Block contact in the reader's toolbar act on the conversation the same way, as the row's own actions and a selection already did; the actions on one message inside the conversation still act on that message alone, and with conversation view off the reader acts on the open message only.
- The reader closes and goes back to the empty pane as soon as the open message leaves the list: Move to trash, Report spam or phishing, Archive, Not spam, a move to a folder, Snooze, Block contact or Delete forever in the reader, the same actions on its row or in a selection, and dropping it on a sidebar folder. It used to keep showing the message, selected, until the move back to the list had finished, which on a busy deployment could take long enough to look stuck. **Undo** in the toast puts the message back in the list.
