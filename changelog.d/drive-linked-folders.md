### Added

- Drive can show a folder from Nextcloud, ownCloud or a NAS as a linked folder: browse, upload, download, rename, move and delete its files, and save attachments into it. Apply the database update from the admin overview first; see [Drive](https://github.com/midego1/kite/blob/main/docs/drive.md#linked-folders-webdav).
