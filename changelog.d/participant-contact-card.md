### Added

- The people in a conversation's header are now buttons. Hovering or focusing one shows the full address (and how many more addresses the same person wrote from); clicking or pressing Enter opens a small contact card with the address and a Copy button, **New email** (the composer opens addressed to them), **Show all mail from** (searches `from:` their address), **Add to contacts** or **Edit contact**, and **Block contact**. Your own address says "You" and offers only Copy. The sender's name in the message opens the same card, and it fits on a phone.
