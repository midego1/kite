### Fixed

- A message you send with an undo delay or schedule for later now appears in Sent at once, instead of nowhere until it went out. Its row says "Sending…" (undo delay) or "Scheduled · Oct 30, 9:00 AM", and a send that failed says "Failed to send". Opening it shows when it goes out; its author can **Cancel send** (it returns to their private Drafts) or **Edit** it in the composer, and a failed one offers **Edit and resend** or **Move to Drafts**. Once it goes out it is an ordinary Sent email. The Sent count, the previous/next arrows in Sent and the conversation view include these messages too. A message waiting to go out cannot be archived, trashed or marked as spam until it is cancelled, since it would be sent anyway.
