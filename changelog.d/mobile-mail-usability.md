### Added

- A floating "New mail" button on phone and foldable screens, so starting a message no longer takes two taps through the menu.
  ![The inbox on a phone, with row actions below each message and a floating compose button above the tab bar](/changelog/2026-11-08-mobile-mail-usability.webp)

### Fixed

- Starring, archiving, trashing, snoozing and marking a message read or unread from the list now work with a tap on any phone, foldable or tablet screen: they no longer needed a mouse hover to even appear.
- The reader's toolbar no longer runs off a narrow phone and hides its own "More actions" button; menus, buttons and sidebar controls across the mail app now keep a comfortably tappable size on a touch screen.
