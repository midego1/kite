### Security

- Mail that fails DMARC from a domain publishing `p=reject` or `p=quarantine` now goes to Spam, even from a saved contact or someone you have written to, and so does mail failing both SPF and DKIM without a DMARC result. A failure from a `p=none` domain, or one whose policy Kite cannot learn, still only adds to the spam score. Any failed SPF, DKIM or DMARC check now cancels the trust a known sender otherwise gets, so a forged From no longer reaches the Inbox on that trust (adapted from upstream Mailflare #216). Kite reads the policy from the receiving server's trusted results or SES, else from the domain's `_dmarc` record (cached, 3-second limit).
- New switch **Treat every DMARC failure as spam** under [Admin → General](/general) → Sender authentication, off by default, sends every DMARC failure to Spam. It needs migration `0084_add_spam_strict_dmarc`; apply it from the database card in Admin.
- Self-hosted: the built-in SMTP listener now checks SPF, DKIM and DMARC itself (with the MIT-licensed mailauth library) and records the result under `MAIL_HOSTNAME`, so the spam filter and the policy above apply to mail received on port 25. It adds a `Received` header with the connecting address, allows 100 sessions in total and 10 per address, 50 recipients per message and 60 seconds of silence, and refuses a message over `SMTP_MAX_SIZE` without holding it in memory (adapted from upstream Mailflare #215).
