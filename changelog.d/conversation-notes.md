### Added

- Notes on conversations: internal notes above a conversation, seen by everyone with access to the mailbox and never sent. Pin one to an email, edit or delete your own; read-only members only read them. Ported from upstream Mailflare #237. Needs a database update from the [Admin overview](/admin); see [Notes](https://github.com/midego1/kite/blob/main/docs/conversation-notes.md).
