### Added

- Ask the assistant to add a receipt or invoice to your subscriptions and it prepares a card with the service, plan, amount, billing period and next renewal for you to check and approve; nothing is saved before you approve, and the card offers Undo. A service you already track gets the email as a new document instead of a second subscription.
