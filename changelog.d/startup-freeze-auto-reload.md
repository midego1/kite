### Fixed

- A loading screen that stops responding is reloaded by itself where the browser allows it: Kite's service worker, now registered for every signed-in tab and still without a fetch handler, notices when a visible tab's loading screen gives no sign of life for 10 seconds and reloads it, at most once per tab in two minutes, so you no longer have to press reload yourself. A tab that freezes again within those two minutes keeps the "Kite stopped responding" notice, a hidden tab is never reloaded, and the first load in a browser is not watched yet. In Chrome and Edge the reload lands as soon as the page comes free, because those browsers wait for a script that never stops before they navigate. The server logs `boot.auto_reloaded_after_freeze` or `boot.freeze_not_reloaded` with the phase and step, and the reloaded load's **Details** say it was reloaded automatically. No migration.
